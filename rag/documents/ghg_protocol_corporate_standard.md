# GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard (Revised Edition)
**Governing Bodies:** World Resources Institute (WRI) and World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD).

## Scope Categorization
- **Scope 1 (Direct GHG Emissions):** Occur from sources owned or controlled by the company (e.g., combustion in boilers, furnaces, vehicles).
- **Scope 2 (Electricity Indirect GHG Emissions):** Accounts for GHG emissions from the generation of purchased electricity consumed by the company.
- **Scope 3 (Other Indirect GHG Emissions):** Optional reporting category including supply chain, product lifecycle, and transportation.

## Emission Intensity Normalization
- Normalizing total emissions by production output (e.g., kg CO₂ per ton of steel, or kg CO₂ per finished unit) is required for operational performance tracking across disparate manufacturing scales.
- Where production volume is zero or unavailable, emission intensity cannot be computed mathematically and must be designated as UNKNOWN.
