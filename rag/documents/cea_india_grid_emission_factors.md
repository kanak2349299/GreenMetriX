# Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Baseline Carbon Dioxide Emission Database
**Version:** 19.0 (Published October 2024)  
**Applicability:** Indian Regional Power Grids (Northern, Western, Southern, Eastern, North-Eastern Unified National Grid)

## Key Emission Factors
- **National Weighted Average Grid Emission Factor (Operating Margin):** 0.716 kg CO₂/kWh (0.716 tCO₂/MWh).
- **Combined Margin (CM) Baseline for Clean Energy Projects:** 0.684 kg CO₂/kWh.
- **Transmission and Distribution (T&D) Losses:** Estimated national average of 19.2%.

## Methodological Guidelines
1. When actual direct on-site emissions (Scope 1) are zero for electrified facilities, Scope 2 emissions must be calculated using the location-based CEA baseline grid factor unless contractually bound to Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs).
2. Emission calculations must clearly distinguish between measured meter readings and factor-derived estimates.
