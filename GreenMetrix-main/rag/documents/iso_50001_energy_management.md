# ISO 50001:2018 Energy Management Systems - Requirements with Guidance for Use
**Standard Body:** International Organization for Standardization (ISO)

## Core Principles
1. **Energy Baseline (EnB):** Quantitative reference providing a basis for comparison of energy performance over specified time periods.
2. **Energy Performance Indicators (EnPI):** Measure or unit of energy performance as defined by the organization.
3. **Significant Energy Uses (SEUs):** Energy uses accounting for substantial consumption and offering considerable potential for efficiency improvements (e.g., electric arc furnaces, compressed air systems, motor drives).

## Decarbonization Hierarchy
1. Continuous operational optimization and waste heat recovery.
2. Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) retrofits on heavy inductive motor loads.
3. Power factor correction (>0.95 lagging) to reduce transmission losses.
4. On-site renewable energy integration (rooftop solar PV, biomass cogeneration).
